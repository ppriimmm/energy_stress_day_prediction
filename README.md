# energy_stress_day_prediction
Use energy and weather data to predict a stress day in term of enery consumption

Feature engineering, 
- total_flow : a total of import and export energy
- demand_sum : a total of demand over great britain
- day_of_week : a number 0-6 representing each day of week. 0 = Sunday, 1 = Monday and so on.
- dow_xx : encoding column for each day. A value of 0,1 indicates whether it belongs to that column. For example, 1 in dow_mon means that row is data of Monday
- is_weekend : indicates whether that row is data of weekend
- same_yesterday_demand : energy demand of yesterday
- same_last_week_demand : enerygy demand on the same day last week
- renew_generation : a total energy generated by renewable energy
- stress : indicates whether it is a stress day. Since there is no offial stress day column provided by the official website, the criteria of stress day is demand > 0.85 percentile of final_demand and renew_generation is less than 0.3 percentile of renew_generation

Models
- linear regression, class weight and random forest


Evaluation metric
- Classification metric, average precision score and 95% CI using bootstrap with 1,000 times of resample.
Average precision score is selected to be a main evaluation method because it does not rely on a threshold. It simply rank all probability of prediction descending and calculate precision score every time it reaches stress day.


Summary
The result shows that linear regression and class weight are the most fit model of this project. They achieve a similar average precision score and CI of 0.9 and 0.595 to 1.000 for linear regression and 0.853 and 0.600 to 1.000 for class weight. Random Forest performs worse in this condition as its score is 0.418 and CI of 0.183 to 0.767. A possible of this negative result could be a small number of features and data as random forest is a complex model and may requries a higher number of those 2 factors to reach its best performance.
